[2026-10-10] Feature: Async JWT Auth & BOLA Protection (Branch: feature/async-jwt-auth)
- Implemented `backend/security.py`:
  * Argon2id hashing for user passwords and persistent refresh tokens.
  * Short-lived JWT access tokens (15m expiry) and rotating refresh tokens (7d expiry) with unique `jti` anti-collision identifiers.
  * OAuth2PasswordBearer dependency and strict JWT decoding / claim validation.
  * User retrieval dependency enforcing implicit identity scoping.
- Implemented `backend/routers/auth.py`:
  * Endpoints `/api/auth/register`, `/api/auth/login`, and `/api/auth/refresh`.
  * Comprehensive refresh token rotation mechanism with automatic revocation upon detecting reuse/replay attacks.
- Implemented `backend/routers/users.py`:
  * Endpoints `/api/users/me` (GET and PATCH).
  * Strict Broken Object Level Authorization (BOLA) mitigation: queries are unconditionally locked to the authenticated caller's identity extracted from the JWT.
- Achieved 95.6% test coverage with pytest-asyncio in `tests/test_auth_and_security.py`.

[2026-10-10] Feature: Submaximal Lift Tracking & Strength Forecasts (Branch: feature/tracking-and-metrics)
- Implemented `backend/routers/tracking.py`:
  * POST `/api/tracking/lifts`: Automatically computes composite 1RM via averaged Epley & Brzycki equations and records lift with user-id binding.
  * GET `/api/tracking/history`: Filterable by exercise name, bounded by pagination, strictly filtered by current user.
  * GET `/api/tracking/forecast`: Computes chronological progression slope (kg/week) and calculates 4-week projected strength.
  * DELETE `/api/tracking/lifts/{lift_id}`: Validates ownership before record deletion.
  * Strict BOLA validation: All operations are implicitly scoped to the calling user's JWT identity.
- Achieved 100% test coverage with pytest-asyncio in `tests/test_tracking.py`.
